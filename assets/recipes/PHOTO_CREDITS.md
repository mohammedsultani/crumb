## Before this goes further

Unlike a Pinterest moodboard, this set is genuinely free-to-use, so it can
ship as-is. Two things worth a look before calling the library "done":

1. **Tone consistency — resolved.** The 21 dishes that read brighter/white-plate
   or had other issues (visible restaurant branding/watermarks, wrong-dish
   photos, alcohol-adjacent props) have been re-sourced: egg fried rice,
   Yangzhou fried rice, Mongolian beef, beef and broccoli, chow mein, orange
   chicken, hot and sour soup, chicken Manchurian, chicken biryani, vegetable
   pulao, dal makhani, chana masala, rajma masala, aloo gobi, fried chicken,
   chicken quesadilla, alfredo pasta, chicken nuggets, hot dogs, black bean
   veggie burger, and loaded fries. The black bean veggie burger in particular
   had shown a beef-and-bacon patty — now shows an actual bean/grain patty.
2. **A few searches had thin free-license pools** (dan dan noodles still
   flagged inline above; Yangzhou fried rice, Mongolian beef, and dal
   makhani were re-sourced during the tone-consistency pass and are closer
   now but still close-visual-match rather than exact-dish) — worth a
   second look if any read as "not quite right" at full size.
3. **Aspect ratios are mixed** (mostly landscape, a couple portrait).
   `contentFit="cover"` center-crops for the grid; revisit per-photo framing
   if you want deliberate crop points instead.
